**Date:** 2026-10-27

## What I did

Went through the full F-150 project document (revision 10, 23 October) and the complete file bundle, and put everything in it on the site. Added 22 photos and reference charts, with metadata stripped.

## What I learned

The [grille guard page](/projects/f150-upfit/grille-guard/) still described the 5 October proposal: L-brackets and backing plates for the speakers, band clamps and corner braces for the light bar, and a 1-3/4 in tube. All of that was replaced on 7 October by the speakers' own center bolts, the light bar's own L-brackets on steel ties, and a measured 1.91 in tube. Rebuilding from the source caught it. The same pass carried the frame-rail ground rule onto every new wiring table.
