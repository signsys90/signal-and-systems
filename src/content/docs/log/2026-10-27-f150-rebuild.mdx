### Power, grounds and wire schedule

- **The two wiring rules (21 Oct):** no ground ever on the battery negative post (the 2015+ F-150's charge sensor is on that cable, per Ford SVE Bulletin Q-231), and no grounds on the aluminum cab, bed, fenders, tailgate or radiator support. Amateur radio gets its own battery feed and ground, never shared with the CanTrol.
- **Main power:** two red leads on the 4-position Molex plug, each fused 40A within 2 ft of the battery, on 8 AWG. Each red carries half of 80A, sized at 125% to 50A. At 50A the Whelen chart allows 8 AWG to 15.5 ft, and the runs are about 12 ft. Two black leads of the same gauge go to the Q-231 point or a frame rail. The module case is grounded too.
- **Ignition sense:** J17-7 through a fuse tap. The page records that Ford's body builder guidance advises against fuse panel taps, and that the choice on record is to keep the tap on a slot picked by meter.
- **Fuse blocks:** two Nilight 6-way ATC/ATO blocks near the battery, one for the CanTrol group and one for accessories, the accessory block fed by 10 AWG.
- **Wire schedule:** 75 rows grouped by device, each with source, destination, wire, protection and notes.
- **Load budget:** about 7.4A of known loads (visor under 2.4A, Color Flood 3.3A max, U-Lites 0.70A, Z-6 heads under 0.5A each as an estimate), with the BooYu, Putco and rocker bars still to measure.
- **Amplifier mount:** under the driver-side rear seat (8 Oct).

### Install order and test plan

Five phases, each tested before the next one hides wires: Phase 0 before touching the truck, Phase 1 bench tests on 12V with no drilling, Phase 1b mock-up on the guard, Phase 2 truck power and mounting, Phase 3 the EFM 3.0 in the front passenger footwell, and Phase 4 final wiring and the full test. The bench section has the power supply kit and the bench wiring for each product (A to D), with the U-Lites done 10 Oct and the Color Flood bar done 11 Oct.

### Install parts and shopping list

Everything bought with the per-item price paid, and everything still to buy in buying order: guard mock-up and mount hardware, lights and modules, wiring day power, fuses, wire and terminals, mounting and shop labor, and install tools. Then install quantities by group. Prices marked "est." are estimates, lengths are estimates for a SuperCrew, and running totals are kept in my spreadsheet and not published.

### Siren speakers

Two UBL 100W speakers, 11 Ω each, in parallel for exactly the CanTrol's 5.5 Ω minimum, so the label impedance must be read before wiring (an 8 Ω rating would put two speakers below Whelen's minimum). Each bolts straight through an existing punch plate hole on its own 1/4"-28 center bolt, with a rubber washer in front and a fender washer, nylon-insert lock nut and blue thread locker behind. The heads are stamped 8.8, so a nut gets test-spun before buying the set. An anti-rotation fix is a suggestion, not yet tested.

### UBL U-Lite grille lights

Two amber/white U-Lites (UBL-UL2-SM) in the upper punch plate corners on #6-32 black stainless screws with #8 fender washers behind the plate. Fed from J1-4 through a 3A in-line fuse within 12 in of the output (0.70A max for both), yellows joined for sync and one white lead used so they alternate. Bench-tested 10 Oct.

### Visor light bar

SpeedTech Raptor Elite Striker-TIR split visor bar, amber/amber, on SpeedTech's Q-RVF15 F-150 brackets, installed 18 Oct with the cable along the headliner and A-pillar. It runs from a 12V socket plug today and moves to J1-2 through the Grand Control Box, which is required for patterns, on wiring day.

### Rear traffic advisor

BooYu 8-head amber traffic advisor on SpeedTech headrest brackets, installed. Its controller needs the power button pressed every time it gets power, so it stays manual and J1-3 only cuts its power. The page also documents the Whelen Dominator Plus as a possible replacement that was not bought.

### Rocker panel lights

Two UBL 60" amber/white running board bars (UBL-TGB-AW2, SAE J595 Class 1) on one 30A relay, with the relay coil on LED Output 3 (slide 3). Not bought yet. The draw isn't listed by UBL, so it gets measured before fuses are chosen, along with the sync steps.

### Putco Work Blade (tailgate)

Putco 60" amber/white Work Blade with the Wireless Remote Kit V3, on 3M tape on top of the rear bumper under the tailgate. CanTrol J1-5 powers it on an amber pattern, and the remote's A button gives white override while B steps patterns. The remote is on constant power, so its standby draw gets measured against Ford's 28 mA key-off guideline.

### Rear plate Z-6 and brake pop

A SpeedTech plate bracket (12.5 x 5.75 in, per SpeedTech support) and two Z-6 TIR brake heads, amber/clear, on LED Outputs 12 and 13. The heads' red brake wire gives steady red while braking, read over OBD-II with the Whelen CC5K1 CANport kit. A front Super Takedown version is planned for later. The page has the Z-6 dimensions, wire charts and color charts from SpeedTech's manuals.

### Factory lamps (EFM 3.0)

The Speed Turtle EFM 3.0 installed inline at BCM J3 and J7 in the front passenger footwell on 8 Oct, firmware v3.36, flashing the factory lamps through the BCM with no taps on factory circuits. F150LEDs CREE headlight, running and turn, and marker bulbs fitted, flashing clean with no hyperflash. The voltage input harness that lets the CanTrol trigger it is still to buy.

### CF-33 laptop mount

The Toughbook CF-33 programs both the CanTrol and the EFM over USB. A passenger-seat mount with a locking keyboard dock and hardwired power is planned, waiting on a used RAM Mounts setup. Fuse and wire come from the mount's manuals once chosen.

## Rules applied on every page

- **My documents first.** Where a document disagrees with an imported chat, the document wins. Where I gave a later instruction, the instruction wins. Every remaining disagreement is listed on the decisions page.
- **Grounds:** the side of a steel frame rail or the Ford Q-231 point on every wiring table. Never battery negative and never the aluminum body.
- **Final mounts:** the 7 Oct decisions (speaker center bolt, light bar on its own L-brackets with ties, U-Lites on #6-32). The 5 Oct proposal hardware is marked superseded.
- **Labels:** Confirmed, Estimate, Unconfirmed or inferred, and Superseded. Anything not confirmed says so or reads TBD.
- **No invented specs.** Unlisted draws, IP ratings and fuse ratings are open items, not guesses.
- **Prices:** per-item prices paid are shown. Running and group totals are not.

## What I learned

- **Rebuild from the source, not from the last page.** The grille guard page looked complete, but it was a faithful copy of a plan I had already replaced. Only reading the source again caught it.
- **Keep the superseded plans visible.** A "Dropped" list and a superseded table stop anyone buying the old parts, and show why the plan changed.
- **One rule, every table.** Writing the ground rule onto every wiring table, instead of one note on the overview, is what makes it hard to get wrong on wiring day.
- **Labels beat hedging.** Confirmed, Estimate, Inferred and Superseded say exactly how much to trust each line.
- **Small change first.** Shipping the header swap as its own PR let it go live in six minutes while the big rebuild was still being written.
