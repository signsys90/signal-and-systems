## What changed

On 27 October I decided to replace the BooYu rear traffic advisor with a **Strobes N' More E68Xtreme LED Stick (8 Module)** in red, amber, white, white, white, white, amber, red, mounted inside the rear window on a **Federal Signal SLB-FRD15ND No-Drill Mount Kit**. Neither is bought yet. The used Whelen Dominator Plus option from 11 October is dropped.

## What's confirmed and what isn't

<Callout type="unverified">
Confirmed from the seller page and my cart: 8 heads, 35.5 x 1.125 x 2 in, 12V DC, 44 in cable, two 2 in brackets, flood and spot optics, needs an external flasher, 5-year warranty that excludes heads run steady. Not confirmed: the stick's wire colors and ground arrangement, its current draw (0.9A per head is taken from the single E6 head spec), how its brackets fix to the Federal Signal brackets, and the K9 ground point. The connector pins for each output are derived from the CanTrol guide's pin order.
</Callout>
