**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Project:** [F-150 Emergency Lighting and 12V Upfit](/projects/f150-upfit/), [UBL Color Flood 2.0 grille light bar](/projects/f150-upfit/ubl-color-flood-light-bar/)

## What I did

Published the first component page for the F-150 upfit: the UBL Color Flood 2.0 20" light bar under the grille guard. It lists all eight wires with their CanTrol output, gauge, fuse and ground, plus the Whelen Command settings each output needs.

## What I learned

The bar is the only light in the build that has been bench-tested end to end, so it was the right one to document first. Writing it up also caught a change: the ground moves from "chassis near the guard" to the side of a steel frame rail, because the F-150 body is aluminum and Ford says not to add grounds to it.

## Next step

Run Stage 4 of the CanTrol bench guide with the bar on the real controller, and measure its draw on the red wire.
