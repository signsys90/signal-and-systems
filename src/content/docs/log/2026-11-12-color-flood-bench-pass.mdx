## What changed

| Area | Change | Page |

| Color Flood bar | Passed the CanTrol bench test on 12 Nov. | Rear window | The BooYu stays in the truck, below the E68Xtreme, on J1-3. Added its 28 patterns and 3 arrow modes from my controller video, and a proposed timer to press its POWER key | [Rear window lights](/projects/f150-upfit/rear-traffic-advisor/) |
| Parts | Bought: Whelen BU97LL back-up alarm ($30.58) and Putco Blade Mounting Brackets ($27.89, product page price). Also the 1N4004 diodes ($5.99) and alligator test leads ($4.99) | [Parts list](/projects/f150-upfit/install-parts-list/) |

## Status

- The Color Flood bar's current draw was not measured. Its wiring is sized to the 5A factory fuse.
- The BooYu timer (Phipps FRM01) and driving its heads straight from the CanTrol are proposals. Nothing is bought, wired or tested.
- The BU97LL hasn't arrived. Its wiring plan is a proposal and it gets its own bench test first.
