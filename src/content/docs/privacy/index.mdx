**Analytics.** This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and does not track you across sites. It reports aggregate page views only.

**Cookies.** This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies.

**Contact form.** If you send a message through the contact form, it is delivered to me through a third-party form service, and I use it only to reply to you.

**Photos and examples.** Photos are published with location data removed, and plates and house numbers blurred. Screenshots and examples of the Responder ID Scanner use synthetic sample IDs only.

**Emergency lighting.** Emergency lighting and siren use is regulated by state law and permits. Lighting pages on this site document a personal build and are not legal guidance.

**Radio and emergency communications.** This site does not publish operational details, frequencies or plans that the relevant agency has not made public.
