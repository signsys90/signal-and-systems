## Before any page goes live

- [ ] Sources linked for every spec.
- [ ] Anything not from a manufacturer page or manual is flagged Unverified.
- [ ] Nothing is marked tested or verified unless it was.
- [ ] No private data (see below).
- [ ] Status and changelog updated, with a build-log link.
- [ ] Alt text written for every image.

## Never publish

- Street addresses, phone number, VIN, license plate, permit or application numbers.
- Anything that shows where the truck is parked overnight.
- Real license or ID data. Scanner examples use synthetic sample IDs only.
- Client names, screenshots or internal data from past employers. Describe the role, the methods and approved resume metrics only.
- ARES or ESF-2 operational details, frequencies or plans the agency has not published. The callsign KR4JSQ is public and fine to show.

## Photos

- Strip EXIF and GPS data before upload.
- Blur plates and house numbers.

## Emergency lighting pages

Every lighting page carries this disclaimer:

> Emergency lighting and siren use is regulated by state law and permits. This page documents a personal build and is not legal guidance.

## Pre-launch check

- [ ] Phone and desktop view.
- [ ] WCAG AA contrast on every color pairing.
- [ ] EXIF stripped from every image.
- [ ] No broken links.
- [ ] Page speed check.
