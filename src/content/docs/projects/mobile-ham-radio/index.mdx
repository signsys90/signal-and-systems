## Goal

Put a usable 2 m / 70 cm amateur radio station in the truck, with an external antenna, without sharing power or grounds with the emergency lighting system.

## Constraints

- 2017 F-150 Lariat SuperCrew with a panoramic roof. The antenna mounts on the rear passenger door instead of the roof.
- The radio must stay electrically separate from the Whelen CanTrol WC system.
- The callsign is KR4JSQ.

## Architecture

```text
BaoFeng BF-F8HP (console) ── SMA jumper ── PL-259 ── Comet HD-5NMO coax ── NMO mount (rear passenger door, top rear corner) ── 7.5" dual-band antenna
```

## Wiring or configuration

The radio runs on its own battery pack, so it shares nothing with the truck's electrical system. If it is ever powered from the truck, it gets its own fused battery feed and its own ground. It never uses the CanTrol fuse block, and it never grounds to the battery negative post.

## Build steps

1. Mount the Comet HD-5NMO at the top rear corner of the rear passenger-side door.
2. Fit the antenna on the NMO base.
3. Route the coax to the console. Keep it away from lighting and siren wiring, and cross any wiring at right angles.
4. Connect the radio to the coax with a short SMA jumper.

## Safety notes

- Keep coax clear of door hinges and pinch points.
- After the warning system is installed, key the radio with the lights and siren running and listen for interference.
