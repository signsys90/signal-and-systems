## Goal

When the SDS200 is mounted face-up in a center console, its 3.5-inch color display faces the roof, and light from roof glass, a sunroof and side windows washes out the screen. The goal is a three-sided hood that bonds to the bezel around the display only, shades the screen, and leaves the keypad, soft keys and knob fully usable.

"Done" means a shroud printed from measured dimensions, installed, and confirmed readable from the driver's seat on a bright midday drive.

## Constraints

- **Nothing drilled into the radio.** The shroud bonds to the bezel with tape and can be removed.
- **Heat.** Closed vehicle interiors exceed PLA's softening temperature, so the part is specified in ASA (preferred), ABS, or PETG at minimum.
- **Clearance.** It must clear the console lid, shifter, cupholders and occupants' knees, and stay out of any airbag deployment path.
- **Unpublished dimensions.** Uniden publishes the chassis size (1.5x DIN-E, 180 x 75 mm) and the 3.5-inch display size, but not the display-window or bezel dimensions, so the design is fully parametric.

## Architecture

A wedge-shaped hood on a thin bonding flange:

- **Far wall, 45 mm tall**, on the side away from the viewer, blocks the main overhead light path.
- **Side walls taper from 45 mm to 10 mm** toward the viewer, blocking side-window light while staying low near the driver.
- **12° outward flare** on each side wall keeps the off-axis viewing angle open.
- **Open near side** faces the viewer. An optional low lip can be added for stiffness.
- **6 x 2 mm flange** bonds to the bezel around the display.

## Wiring or configuration

There is no wiring. Configuration is the model's parameters, edited in OpenSCAD (`SOURCE/sds200_glare_shroud.scad`) or passed to the Python generator.

| Parameter | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `win_w` | 76 mm | 40 to 120 | Opening width. Visible display width plus about 2 mm. Estimate. |
| `win_h` | 58 mm | 30 to 100 | Opening height. Visible display height plus about 2 mm. Estimate. |
| `flange` | 6 mm | 3 to 10 | Bonding land. Narrowest flat bezel distance minus 0.5 mm, max 8. Estimate. |
| `flange_t` | 2.0 mm | 1.6 to 3 | Flange thickness. |
| `wall_t` | 2.4 mm | 1.6 to 3.2 | Wall thickness. Use multiples of the nozzle width. |
| `h_far` | 45 mm | 20 to 60 | Far-wall height. Overhead clearance minus 5 mm, max 60. |
| `h_near` | 10 mm | 0 to 25 | Side-wall height at the viewer end. |
| `flare` | 12° | 0 to 20 | Outward lean of the side walls. More means a wider viewing angle. |
| `near_lip` | 0 mm | 0 to 6 | Optional lip on the open side. 0 is fully open. |
| `part` | `shroud` | `shroud` or `gauge` | Production part or 2 mm fit gauge. |

Python generator, for anyone without OpenSCAD:

```text
pip install trimesh manifold3d numpy
python SOURCE/generate_stl.py --win-w 78 --win-h 60 --flange 5 --out out
```

## Build steps

1. **Measure the radio** with calipers, to 0.5 mm: visible display width and height, the narrowest flat bezel land on all four sides, and overhead clearance in the console. Note which side faces the driver and where the strongest light comes from.
2. **Optional cardboard mockup.** The instructions PDF has a 1:1 template (Appendix B) to cut from card and test on a bright drive before printing.
3. **Set the parameters** from the measurements and render.
4. **Print the fit gauge** (`part = "gauge"`, about 15 to 20 minutes). The display must be fully visible and the flange must sit flat without touching any button. Adjust and repeat until it fits.
5. **Print the shroud** (`part = "shroud"`) to the settings above.
6. **Inspect:** flange flat within about 0.3 mm, opening within ±0.3 mm of target, no layer splits at the wall-to-flange joints, no stringing inside, matte inner faces.
7. **Post-process:** remove the brim and deburr. Optionally scuff the inner faces with 400-grit or line them with flocking paper. Do not paint the flange underside.
8. **Install:** clean the bezel and flange with isopropyl alcohol, dry-fit with the tall wall toward the strongest light and the open side toward the driver, apply tape at least 1 mm back from the inner edge, and press for 30 seconds. VHB needs 24 hours before heat exposure.
9. **Check readability** on a bright midday drive and tune (see the troubleshooting table in the instructions PDF).

To remove it, warm the flange with a hair dryer on low and cut the tape with fishing line or dental floss. Do not pry against the screen.

## Safety notes

- Keep the shroud and radio clear of any airbag deployment path.
- Confirm it does not interfere with the shifter, console lid, cupholders or occupants' knees.
- Do not adjust the radio or shroud while driving. Make changes while parked.
- Deburr all edges so there are no sharp corners.
- Do not print in PLA. It can sag or warp in a hot cab.

## Lessons learned

- Manufacturer specs often stop at the chassis. When the critical dimension is unpublished, design parametrically and add a cheap fit gauge, so a wrong guess costs 5 g of filament instead of a full print.

## Open items

- Measure the display window, bezel land and console clearance on the physical radio.
- Print and test-fit the gauge, then update the defaults.
- Render the OpenSCAD source once and confirm it matches the STLs.
- Print the shroud and record actual material, settings, print time and mass.
- Install, test on a bright drive, and add photos (EXIF stripped).
- Re-release as v1.1 once fit is verified.
