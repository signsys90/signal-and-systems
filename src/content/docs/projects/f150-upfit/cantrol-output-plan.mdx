Ratings and pinouts are from the Whelen CanTrol WC Installation/Operating Guide, form 14580G. Most outputs are set to steady on (Turn On), because those lights run their own flash patterns. The Strobes N' More E68Xtreme stick has no built-in flasher and can't burn steady, so the CanTrol flashes its outputs (phase A or B). The light bar wire functions were confirmed on the bench on 2026-10-11. Nothing on this page has been tested in the truck yet.

The tables below are generated from Upfit Studio, the desktop app that holds the build's wiring and control plan. Every change saved in the app updates them, so they always match the current plan.

<Callout type="unverified">
Guide 14580G shows two different colors for LED Outputs 11 and 12 on different pages. Identify those two leads by pin (J4-15 and J4-16) and with a meter, not by color.
</Callout>

## Control head keys

The Whelen CCTL7 21-button slide head. The slide has four positions; far left is off. Key caps are from a photo of this head; labels on blank keys are the planned functions.

<UpfitStudioPlan show="head" />

Priority when controls overlap, top wins: brake pop, TAKE DOWN, WHITE, STEADY, then the slide. Brake pop is always on: the Z-6 heads and the E68Xtreme red modules flash for about 2 seconds, then the Z-6 heads stay steady red while the brake is held (inferred, not yet bench-tested).

## Power and inputs

- **Main input:** 80A max through two red leads, each fused 40A at the battery, and two black grounds. 12V DC ±20%, negative ground only.
- **Ignition sense:** J17-7 (red/white).
- **Left unconnected:** horn ring (J17-1), Park Sense (J17-2) and backlight (J17-5). Backlight brightness is set in Whelen Command instead.
- **Estimated load:** about 9A worst case with everything on, well under the 40A combined limit. This is an estimate, not a measurement.
