## Goal

Small items that make the truck a working vehicle: an extinguisher within reach, decals that tell responders where it is, and white light in the bed for work at night.

## Fire extinguisher (installed 14 Nov)

- **What:** H3R Performance HalGuard Pro, 2.5 lb clean-agent extinguisher with a pressure gauge and strap bracket. The brand and part number (28131) were read from the label in the product photo and are still to check on the unit.
- **Where:** back of the driver-side headrest, on a quick-detach mount.
- **Care (general knowledge, not from the maker):** check each month that the gauge needle stays in the green and that the quick-detach latch holds firmly over bumps.

## Window decals (installed 14 Nov)

Two pairs of 2 x 2 in waterproof vinyl decals, a green first aid cross and a red fire extinguisher, on the rear passenger windows. They match the extinguisher behind the driver seat.

## LED truck bed lights (bought 14 Nov)

Three 60 in 12V 6500K white COB LED strips with an IP67 housing, a splitter cable, a blade fuse, an on/off switch and mounting hardware, per the listing. This is work lighting, not a warning light.

<Callout type="unverified" title="Still to confirm">
The listing gives no current draw or wire colors. I'll measure the draw with the AstroAI meter before picking the feed and fuse, and check the colors on arrival. Placement on the bed rails and the power source aren't decided yet. The ground goes to a Ford factory ground point or the frame rail, never the battery negative post.
</Callout>
