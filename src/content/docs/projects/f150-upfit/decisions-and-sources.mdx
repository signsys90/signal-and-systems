## Source rule

My own documents are treated as most accurate. Where they disagree with an imported chat, the document wins. Where I gave a later instruction (6 Oct: U-Lites in the upper punch plate, light bar under the lower tube; 7 Oct: light bar on its own L-brackets with stainless ties), that instruction wins. Every remaining disagreement is listed below.

Status labels used across these pages:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed | Stated in my documents, a manufacturer data sheet, or checked against a product page |
| Estimate | Typical price, a figure read off a photo or drawing, or an estimate awaiting a tape measure |
| Unconfirmed / inferred | An assumption or inference that still needs checking |
| Superseded | Replaced by a later or more authoritative source. Kept for the record only |

## Conflicts between sources

| Topic | Source A | Source B | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Head location | Design brief: overhead ranked 1 and 2 | Config sheet: no overhead mount (sunroof) | Resolved 21 Oct: overhead console on the Ben3DBuild mount, in the sunglasses holder opening |
| Center console | Parts log and config sheet: "no console" | Design brief: flow-through console with armrest bin | Both true: the truck has the factory console, but no equipment console |
| Ground point | Project document: bare chassis near the guard; UBL manuals: battery negative | Ford SVE Bulletin Q-231 and Ford body builder guidance | Ford wins: Q-231 point or the side of a steel frame rail, never battery negative or aluminum body |
| Roof | Project document: "sunroof" | My standing notes: panoramic roof | Either way, nothing mounts on the roof |
| U-Lite rear washer | Diagram: #6 stainless fender washer | Hardware table: black stainless #8, 1-1/4 in across | Table used; it must span the punch hole |
