## Goal

Install everything in an order where each phase is tested before the next one buries wires, so a fault is always found on the bench or in the open rather than behind trim. Done means every phase below is checked off and every row of the control behavior table works in the truck.

## Constraints

- Bench test every product on 12V before drilling or mounting.
- Make every bench connection with the supply unplugged.
- Never connect a siren speaker straight to 12V, and never run a siren tone with no speaker connected.
- The Speed Turtle EFM 3.0 can't be bench-tested. It only works plugged into the truck's BCM.
- The Whelen 295HFSA6 is a bench-test unit only. It is never installed on the truck.

## Phase 1: bench test on 12V (no drilling)

The CanTrol has its own step-by-step guide: the [CanTrol bench test plan](/projects/f150-upfit/cantrol-bench-test/) (Gen 3 bench test guide, 23 Oct).

- [ ] Power the module (fuse per Whelen), connect the head and mic, check each output LED for shorts.
- [ ] Connect one speaker at a time; play every tone in short bursts at low volume, people 5 ft back.
- [ ] Program the output plan over USB; save and back up.
- [ ] Slide positions 1/2/3: confirm J1 Outputs 1 to 4 and LED Outputs 3 and 4.
- [x] Note the BooYu power-on behavior: it needs its button pressed every time (10 Oct).
- [ ] Visor bar through the Grand Control Box: dim, steady, pattern.
- [ ] Rocker bars: measure draw steady and flashing, size fuse and wire, practice sync.
- [x] Color Flood bar: modes, arrows and pattern wire (11 Oct). U-Lites alternate (10 Oct).
- [ ] Measure the BooYu and rocker draws while each product is on the bench.

### Fuse for each bench test

| Test | Fuse in the holder | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One U-Lite at a time | 5A (smallest in the kit) | About 0.35A each; on the truck it gets 3A |
| Color Flood 2.0 bar | 5A | 3.3A max |
| CanTrol + one speaker | 20A | Short, low-volume siren bursts only |
| 295HFSA6 + one speaker | 20A | Unit label: 8A typical with one 100W speaker, 16A with two |

Check each light's instructions before tapping a pattern wire. On some it goes to ground, not +12V.

### A. Set up the power supply (once)

1. Unplugged, wire the VSEER cord to the supply's AC terminals: black to L, white to N, green to the ground symbol. Close the clear terminal cover.
2. Screw one lead of a Nilight fuse holder into a V+ terminal. Screw a short black lead or clip into a V− terminal.
3. Clip the red KAIWEETS lead to the fuse holder's other end, and the black KAIWEETS lead to V−.
4. Pull the fuse, plug in, and check the supply's green LED is on. Unplug before every rewire.

### B. UBL U-Lite

Fuse 5A. Red clip to red (Mode 1), black clip to black. Wire-nut blue, green, white and yellow. Plug in: it flashes. Yellow to +12V for 1 s steps patterns; 3 to 5 s steps back. Optionally check Modes 2 and 3 on blue and green, then both lights together with yellows joined and one white on +. Full steps on the [U-Lite page](/projects/f150-upfit/ubl-u-lite-grille-lights/).

### C. UBL Color Flood 2.0 bar

Fuse 5A. Red alone on the red clip, black on the black clip, every other wire capped separately. Red alone does nothing. Touch brown, orange, then white to +12V for Modes 1 to 3; blue and green for the arrows; yellow for pattern, color and steady. Never join red to a mode wire. Full steps and results on the [Color Flood page](/projects/f150-upfit/ubl-color-flood-light-bar/).

### D. Whelen CanTrol WC

1. Fuse 20A. Twist both red power wires together into the fuse holder's lead (no clip). Put both black ground wires on V−. With one red left off, part of the unit can stay dead and look like a fault.
2. Plug the control head cable into the module, and the mic into the module's 1/4 in mic jack.
3. Speaker: the module's orange speaker wire to the speaker's white (+), brown to its black (−). One speaker only on the bench.
4. Plug in. Check the head lights up and each output's indicator LED shows no short.
5. Run each siren tone in short, low-volume bursts. Try the PA mic.
6. Optional output check: one U-Lite's red on an output (for example J1-4), black on V−. The light comes on when that output is selected on the head.
7. Connect the Toughbook by USB and confirm Whelen Command sees the unit. Save the existing program before changing it.

**Ignition sense:** also put the red/white ignition wire (J17-7) on V+ through the fuse holder. Without it the system stays off.

### E. Whelen 295HFSA6 (bench unit only)

1. Fuse 20A. Both heavy red leads twisted together into the fuse holder (mine have no tags), black to V−.
2. Speaker: yellow to the speaker's + (white), brown to its − (black). Wire-nut orange (the second-speaker output).
3. Wire-nut the optional wires: blue (radio), violet (siren shut-down), white and grey (horn relay).
4. Optional: white/yellow to +12V turns on the panel backlight.
5. Turn the power switch on. Set the rotary knob to RAD, press and release MAN to run SI-TEST: the diagnostic light stays steady about 1.5 s if there's no fault. A single flash on the speaker LED means a speaker short.
6. Try the siren tones in short, low-volume bursts.
7. Optional output check: one U-Lite's red to terminal 1, black to V−. With factory settings it comes on in slide position 1 (position 2: terminals 1 and 2; position 3: terminals 1 to 5).

## Phase 1b: mock-up on the guard

UBL parts and JOUGU ties arrived 10 October. Fit everything to the guard on the ground before the permanent mounting, hand-tight only, no thread locker, tape in place of the steel ties, and protect the new paint.

- [ ] Speakers: at least two threads past the nut, or note a longer bolt in the same thread.

- [ ] Amplifier on its floor plate under the driver-side rear seat, with air above the fins. Head on its mount, clear of airbags and the driver's knee.

- [x] Q-RVF15 bracket and visor bar, cable along the headliner and A-pillar (18 Oct).
- [x] BooYu traffic advisor and controller.
- [ ] Rocker bars low on the sills; relay and fuses; LED Output 3 to the coil.

## Phase 3: Speed Turtle EFM 3.0 (front passenger footwell)

- [x] Installed inline at BCM J3 and J7 (8 Oct).
- [ ] Switch it to voltage input: fit the Voltage Input Harness.
- [ ] White to the LED Output 4 wire, green to the LED Output 5 wire.
- [ ] Battery on; program with the STE utility; test white, green, both and double-pull; confirm brake, turn and reverse still work.

## Phase 4: final wiring and full test

- [ ] Crimped connectors, loom, grommets; away from heat, fans and moving parts.
- [ ] Insert fuses one at a time.
- [ ] Run every row of the [control behavior table](/projects/f150-upfit/cantrol-output-plan/).
- [ ] Low-speed private drive: chafing, factory lights normal, light bar ties and grille hardware tight.
- [ ] Re-check connections and mounting bolts after the first week.
- [ ] Photograph every fuse location and the CanTrol settings before closing up.
