## Goal

Two small amber/white warning lights high on the grille guard, flashing in sync and alternating left and right, run from one CanTrol output. Done means both are mounted on the punch plate with machine screws, wired through a 3A fuse within 12 in of the output, and working from the slide switch in the truck.

## Constraints

- **Location:** upper left and upper right corners of the punch plate (decided 6 Oct; the first proposal had them in the lower corners).
- **No drilling.** Each light is centered over an existing punch plate hole, so its leads run straight back through it.
- **Fuse:** UBL calls for a 3A fuse within 12 in of the power source.
- **Ground:** frame rail side or the Ford Q-231 point, never the battery negative post.

## Wire functions

| Lead | Function (UBL manual) | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red | Mode 1: powers the light in Mode 1 | Both reds to J1-4 through the 3A fuse |
| Blue | Mode 2 | Capped, each separately |
| Green | Mode 3 | Capped, each separately |
| Yellow | Pattern / Sync | The two yellows joined for sync. A brief +12V steps the pattern |
| White | Alternate | Left light's white on J1-4 after the fuse, so the pair alternates. Right light's white capped |
| Black | Ground | Both to a frame rail ground |

**Patterns:** +12V on yellow for 1 second steps to the next of 40 patterns. 3 to 5 seconds steps back.

## Mount (final, 7 Oct)

- Two #6-32 x 3/4 in black stainless machine screws per light, through the light's tabs and the punch plate. UBL said #6-32 fits the holes best. If one is loose at the mock-up, use #8-32.
- Black stainless #8 fender washers, 1-1/4 in across, behind the plate. The washer must span the punch hole, so measure one hole first and go to 1-1/2 in if the hole is over 1 in.
- Stainless #6-32 nylon-insert lock nuts behind the washers.
- The supplied screws are pan-head self-tappers, about 1/2 in long, meant for 1/16 in pilot holes. They fit the housing snugly (checked 10 Oct) but take no nut, so they're not used.
- Until wiring day, the 6 in leads stay tucked behind the plate with taped ends.

Full hardware and prices are on the [grille guard](/projects/f150-upfit/grille-guard/) page.

## Bench test

![Bench wiring for one U-Lite: the SUPERNIGHT 12V supply through a 5A fuse to the red lead, black to ground, the other leads wire-nutted.](../../../../assets/f150/bench-b-ulite.png)

*Bench setup B from the project document.*

1. Fuse 5A (the smallest in the bench kit; the truck gets 3A). Red clip to the light's red (Mode 1), black clip to black.
2. Wire-nut blue, green, white and yellow separately.
3. Plug in. The light should flash. Touch yellow to +12V for 1 s to step patterns; 3 to 5 s steps back.
4. Optional: move the red clip to blue, then green, to check Modes 2 and 3.
5. Both lights together: both reds on +, both blacks on −, the yellows joined so they sync. Put one light's white on + and they alternate.
6. Repeat for the second light.
