## Goal

One list for the whole build, so nothing is forgotten on wiring day and nothing gets bought twice. Wire IDs in brackets match the [wire schedule](/projects/f150-upfit/power-and-wiring/).

Prices are what I paid, before tax and shipping unless noted. Prices marked "est." are estimates, not checked store prices. Lengths are estimates for a SuperCrew; buy with some spare. Running totals are kept in my project spreadsheet and aren't published here.
