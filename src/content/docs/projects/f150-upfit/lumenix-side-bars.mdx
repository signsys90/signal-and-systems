## Goal

Side warning on both sides of the bed, low and out of the way under the tonneau cover. Done means both pairs are mounted on the bed rails, run from one controller on one CanTrol output, flash in sync, and pass a draw and heat check on the bench.

## Current build (18 Nov 2026)

- **Bars:** LUMENIX LED Traffic Advisor Strobe Light Bar with Controller, 2 x 19 in, Amber White. Two pairs, four bars.
- **Where:** pair 1 on the driver-side bed rail behind the cab, pair 2 on the passenger-side bed rail behind the cab. Both are already mounted.
- **Path:** 12V into pair 1's inline controller, then one small 3-pin 2-way splitter to pair 1 and pair 2. Pair 2's own controller and cigarette plug go in the parts bin as spares.
- **Power:** the cigarette plug comes off and the lead is fed from CanTrol J1 Output 3 (10A). It's on in slide positions 1, 2 and 3.
- **Pattern:** the controller's own memory recall. There's no CanTrol pattern wire.

How it got here: on 16 Nov I bought the first pair and planned one bar per bed rail. On 17 Nov I approved an 8-bar plan. On 18 Nov I set the current build back to 2 pairs and kept the 8-bar layout as a future option only.

## Mount hardware (bought 16 Nov)

- Scotch VHB Extreme Mounting Tape, 60 in.
- #8 x 3/8 in flat head screws for the tight C-channel on the bed rail.
- #10 lock washers. These are loose on a #8 screw, so what each is for still needs confirming.

## Bench checks before wiring

- Draw of all four bars on one controller, measured in series. The J1-3 fuse is picked from it.
- A 10-minute heat check on the controller box.
- Both pairs flash in sync.
- The splitter matches the LUMENIX plug.

## Future option, not planned now

Four pairs, eight bars, on the same controller through a 3-way splitter, an extension and a 2-way splitter. Pairs 3 and 4 would wrap the rear corners: bar A on the bed cap end facing out and bar B on the tailgate corner facing rear (wiring diagram Rev 2, approved 17 Nov, not built or tested).
