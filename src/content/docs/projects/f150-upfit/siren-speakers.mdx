## Goal

Two siren speakers on the front of the truck, driven by the Whelen CanTrol WC's built-in siren amplifier, mounted without drilling and aimed straight ahead. Done means both are bolted to the punch plate, wired in parallel with matched polarity, every tone is tested at low volume, and the impedance is read off the labels.

## Constraints

- **Load:** the CanTrol accepts one or two 100W speakers with a 5.5 Ω minimum total load (guide 14580G). Two 11 Ω speakers in parallel make exactly 5.5 Ω, so the label impedance must be confirmed before wiring. An 8 Ω rating would put two speakers below Whelen's minimum.
- **Mount:** no drilling on the guard. Each speaker uses its own center bolt through an existing punch plate hole.
- **Speakers only connect to an amplifier's speaker output.** Never to 12V.
- **Certification:** Whelen certifies SAE, California Title 13 and NFPA compliance with Whelen speakers. Third-party speakers work but may not carry that certification.

## Mount (final, 7 Oct)

No L-bracket. With its flange bolted flat to the plate, the speaker would aim up or down. Instead, the speaker's own center bolt goes straight through the punch plate.

```text
SIDE SECTION, one speaker (front of truck to the left)

  Speaker ── rubber/neoprene washer ── stainless flat washer ── PUNCH PLATE ── fender washer 1/4 x 1-1/4 OD ── 1/4"-28 nyloc + blue Loctite
             (existing hole, no drilling)                                     at least 2 threads past the nut
```

1. Spin a 1/4"-28 nut onto one speaker bolt at the store before buying the set. The heads are stamped 8.8, a metric-style marking, so the thread needs checking even though UBL says 1/4"-28.
2. Offer the speaker to its bay, above the lower horizontal tube against the outside of each center vertical tube, and confirm it fits.
3. Stack in front of the plate: rubber washer against the speaker, then the flat washer.
4. Behind the plate: fender washer, then the nyloc with blue thread locker. At least two threads must show past the nut; if not, use a longer 1/4"-28 bolt.
5. Re-check after the first drives. One center bolt can let a speaker twist on a vibrating guard. If it turns, add a second fastener or a tab that bears on a mesh hole (suggestion, not yet tried).

## Wiring

The CanTrol's siren amp has two speaker leads: orange (high) and brown (low). Both speakers are wired in parallel with matched polarity.

The 16 AWG runs go through the grille in split loom, through a firewall grommet, and back to the module under the driver-side rear seat. Extend the module's own orange and brown leads with 16 AWG only if they're short.

<Callout type="safety" title="Never on 12V">

</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause and fix |
| --- | --- |
| Siren low or distorted | Impedance below 5.5 Ω total, a weak feed, out-of-phase wiring, or a speaker facing a blocked surface |
| Slide position 3 also makes a wail | A tone is assigned to position 3 in Whelen Command. Remove it and re-save |

## Safety notes

- Test at low volume with people at least 5 ft from the speaker.
- Hearing protection for anyone close during tests (general knowledge).
- Grommet every metal pass-through.

## Open items

- Confirm the 1/4"-28 thread and that two threads show past the nut.
- Confirm each speaker fits its punch plate bay.
- Read the label impedance on both speakers.
- Pick the sticker design.
