## Goal

Rear warning low on the truck, plus a bright white backup flood. Done means both lights are mounted under the rear bumper, the sides flash with the warning slide, the center white comes on in reverse and with REAR FLOOD, and pattern and color can be changed from a key fob without giving up any control head key.

## Placement

Two units upside down under the rear bumper, one each side of the hitch, facing back. Before buying, check the spot against the hitch, the parking sensors, the exhaust and the spare tire hoist.

Blue and white leads are capped. If the bench shows the lights can't follow a CanTrol flash pattern cleanly, they run their own pattern in all slides instead.

The same 4-channel remote also runs the [HELLA heads](/projects/f150-upfit/hella-quad-color-heads/): CH2 for color and CH3 for pattern. CH4 is spare.

## Bench checks

- Current draw of both sides together (over 8A means a solid-state relay on J1-4).
- LC AUX 2 polarity, and both yellows together under 250 mA.
- Pattern memory after power off.
- No backfeed between the Putco white override and the Mini-X greens on J1 Output 8.

## UBL X-Lite 2X dash light (backburner)

On 16 Nov I planned a UBL X-Lite 2X dash light (UBL-XL-2X), low center on the windshield and clear of the airbag, red on LED Output 25, with a 2-channel 433 MHz relay remote on J17-3 for pattern and color. On 18 Nov I put it on the backburner and the Mini-X took its place. It stays a future option: it's out of the to-buy total and its outputs (LED 25, LC AUX 2, J17-3) were released, then LC AUX 2 and J17-3 went to the Mini-X.
