## Goal

A forward-facing amber warning bar inside the windshield that comes on in every slide position, with no roof mount (the truck has a panoramic roof). Done means the bar is hardwired to J1-2 through the Grand Control Box, its patterns work from the control head, and the cable is hidden along the headliner and A-pillar.

## Constraints

- **No roof mounts.** The bar sits inside the windshield on SpeedTech's vehicle-specific visor brackets.
- **Patterns need the Grand Control Box.** SpeedTech's manual says patterns are "only functional when using in conjunction with STL Grand Control Box."
- **Fuse:** keep SpeedTech's own fuse, no larger.
- **Airbags:** the cable runs along the headliner and A-pillar. Keep it behind the curtain airbag, never across its deployment path, and refit the pillar trim clips the way Ford designed them.
- **Steady burn:** SpeedTech says not to run steady burn more than an hour.

## Wire functions (SpeedTech manual)

| Wire | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Red | +12V |
| Black | Ground |
| White | Flash Mode 1 |
| Brown | Flash Mode 2 (only while Mode 1 is on) |
| Yellow | Pattern |
| Green | Take Down, steady |
| Yellow/black | Super Take Down, steady |
| Blue | Dim |

Today those wires all go into the Grand Control Box, and the box's own buttons pick the function.

**Hardwire steps for wiring day:** cut off the 12V socket plug, read the fuse rating that was in it, put a Nilight in-line fuse holder with that same rating on the red wire, join red to the J1-2 wire (V1) and black to ground (V2).

**No-cut alternative:** wire a 12V female socket (about $8, not on the shopping list) to J1-2 and plug the bar's existing plug into it.

## Five-modes decision

The proposed [all-lights five modes plan](/projects/f150-upfit/five-modes-plan/) has an open choice (decision B) for this bar:

- **Direct from the CanTrol:** the Grand Control Box comes out and the CanTrol drives white, brown, green, yellow/black, blue and yellow on LED Outputs 18 to 23, with J1-2 still the +12V. All five modes work with one press. This only happens if a bench test shows the bar runs useful patterns without the box.
- **Keep the Grand Control Box:** simpler. The visor stays on the box's own buttons, outside the one-press modes.

## Bench test

Through the Grand Control Box: dim, steady, pattern (bench test plan, setup for the visor). For the five-modes option: red and white only, then add the rest, and test whether yellow steps patterns with no box.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause and fix |
| --- | --- |
| Flashes but patterns won't change | The Grand Control Box is not in the chain |
| Dead after hardwiring | Fuse in the new holder, J1-2 not programmed for that slide position, or a poor ground |

## Open items

- Read the fuse rating inside the 12V plug before cutting it off.
- Decide five-modes option B: direct from the CanTrol, or keep the Grand Control Box.
