## Goal

Four small, bright heads high on the grille guard's upper punch plate, in the spot the two UBL U-Lites held in the earlier plan. On the road they show amber and white only. Done means all four are on the plate, each color leg is fused and switched by a relay from the CanTrol, the four heads flash in sync, and every function is bench-tested before the guard goes on the truck.

## What changed

- **16 Nov 2026:** I set the lighting roster to four HELLA Quad Color heads on the upper punch plate. They replace the two U-Lites, and LED Outputs 14 to 17 and 24 move to them. The front vanity plate Z-6 option gave those outputs up and is now a future option with no outputs.
- **13 Nov:** seller brackets bought: two single and one double.
- **18 Nov:** wiring Rev 2 (below), with a 433 MHz 4-channel remote for color and pattern steps.
- **19 Nov:** all four heads received (bought used). First bench on the same day.

## First bench, 19 Nov

- **RED is the +12V power wire**, not a red-color wire.
- Red alone, and red with white, gives an amber/white flash.
- Red with orange gives blue/amber. That isn't road-legal, so that group gets reprogrammed.
- Current was **not measured yet**. The wiring below stays as planned until it is.

## Wiring, Rev 2 (18 Nov, proposed)

Each color leg runs through its own relay, with a 2A fuse per head on each leg and a 10A feed on 14 AWG to the relays. The CanTrol only drives the relay coils and two signal wires. CanTrol lead colors on my harness don't follow the Whelen guide, so every CanTrol connection is identified by pin.

The remote is one 433 MHz 4-channel receiver shared with the rear [UBL Mini-X plan](/projects/f150-upfit/ubl-mini-x-rear-bumper/): CH2 closes J17-4 (HELLA color), CH3 closes J17-9 (HELLA pattern), CH1 runs the Mini-X and CH4 is spare. No control head keys change.
