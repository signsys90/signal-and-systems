## Goal

Rear-facing amber warning and directional lighting in the rear window, switched off and on with the rest of the system. Done means the controller is hardwired to J1-3 through a 10A fuse and its behavior is documented.

## Constraints

- **Interior only.** The bar sits in the rear window. The listing shows suction cups; on this truck it's on SpeedTech headrest rear mounting brackets.
- **Its own controller.** The cable between the controller and the bar has a 4- or 5-pin plug (listing photo), too few wires for one per head, so the CanTrol can't run the heads one by one. The CanTrol's J10 traffic advisor port isn't used.
- **Manual power button.** It needs its power button pressed every time it gets power, even after a power cut (confirmed 10 Oct 2026).

## How it's used

It stays manual. I keep the controller within reach and press it on when it's needed. J1-3 still cuts its power with the slide switch, so it can never be left running with the system off. In the five-modes plan it can only do amber flash (M3, and the amber part of M5).

## Possible replacement: Whelen Dominator Plus (not bought)

On 11 October I found a used Whelen 8-head Dominator Plus traffic advisor, all amber (AAAAAAAA), with LIN6 optics and end flashers, at Brooks Government Surplus for $325.00 (listing price, condition A). The listing's example unit is model DPTAW8, PN 01-0688639-F80, LINZ6 Super-LED, 12VDC, made 05/20. Units vary (TADP8RB, TADP8, DPTAW8 and similar).

The two end heads are dedicated alternating flashers and the middle six flash or run built-in traffic advisor patterns. It has four control wires (TA left, TA right, end flash only, TA flash), so the CanTrol only switches it.

## Bench test

Note the power-on behavior (done: it needs its button every time), and measure the draw with a meter in series on red so the 10A fuse can be confirmed.
