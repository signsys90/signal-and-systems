I asked for an "E86" traffic advisor; the project files of 27 October name the part as the Strobes N' More **E68**Xtreme LED Stick, so this page uses that name.

## Goal

Rear-facing warning and directional lighting in the rear window, inside the cab, switched on and off with the rest of the system from the CanTrol. Done means the E68Xtreme is on the Federal Signal brackets, each of its eight heads is flashed by its own CanTrol LED output with flash patterns only, the ground and draw are confirmed on the bench, and the BooYu and its wiring are removed.

## Constraints

- **Interior only.** Both lights sit inside the rear window. Nothing mounts on the roof (panoramic roof).
- **Airbags.** The stick, its 44 in cable and the K1–K9 loom stay out of the curtain airbag paths along the headliner and C-pillars.
- **No drilling.** The Federal Signal kit is a no-drill bracket for the 2015+ F-150. The third brake light and rear headliner come off for access.
- **External flasher required.** The E68Xtreme has no built-in flasher. The seller says it needs an external LED flasher or a full control system. The CanTrol is that flasher.
- **Flash only, never steady.** The seller's warranty excludes heads damaged by running constantly on. Every K output is set to a flash pattern in Whelen Command, never steady.
- **Grounds.** All added grounds go to a steel frame rail side or the Ford Q-231 ground point, never battery negative or the aluminum body (section rule).

### How the CanTrol drives it

The CanTrol is the stick's flasher. Each head gets its own LED output (LED Outputs 6, 7, 8, 27, 28, 29, 30 and 31), each with its own flash pattern and phase set in Whelen Command. That lets the stick run warning patterns, left and right traffic advisor sweeps, and the amber and white modes of the [five-modes plan](/projects/f150-upfit/five-modes-plan/). This is inferred from the outputs' capability; the patterns aren't programmed yet.

| Head | Color | Wire | CanTrol output | Connector pin (derived) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (left end) | Red | K1 | LED Output 6 | J4-8 |
| 2 | Amber | K2 | LED Output 7 | J4-9 |
| 3 | White | K3 | LED Output 8 | J4-10 |
| 4 | White | K4 | LED Output 27 | J2-11 |
| 5 | White | K5 | LED Output 28 | J2-12 |
| 6 | White | K6 | LED Output 29 | J2-13 |
| 7 | Amber | K7 | LED Output 30 | J2-14 |
| 8 (right end) | Red | K8 | LED Output 31 | J2-15 |

The connector pins are derived from the CanTrol guide's pin order (LED 1–16 on J4, LED 17–32 on J2-1 to J2-16 in the same order), not from the project document. Whelen's guide gives two different color sets for some LED outputs (see the [controller page](/projects/f150-upfit/cantrol-controller/)), so identify every lead by pin number and a meter, never by color.

The wire colors, the common ground and the 0.9A per-head draw are all inferred: the seller's page lists no wire colors or current for the stick. Before any wire is landed, put the stick on the bench on 12V through a fused lead, identify each head's lead and the ground with a meter, and measure each head's draw. Each output is limited to 2.5A, so a 0.9A head is well inside it; the eight heads together (about 7.2A max) are split across the J4 and J2 output groups, each fused at 20A on the module, and all outputs together are limited to 40A.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause and fix |
| --- | --- |
| BooYu doesn't light | Controller not switched on, in-line fuse, or a poor ground |
| One E68Xtreme head dark | That head's LED output not programmed or its lead not landed; check the output in Command and the pin with a meter |
| All E68Xtreme heads dark | Common ground (K9) loose or on a painted or aluminum surface; slide switch not in a position that enables the outputs |
| A head stays lit | Output set to steady in Command. Change it to a flash pattern: steady-on damage isn't covered by the seller's warranty |
